## 11. Sprint 2 Priorities

### High-Priority Items

- **US-07 — Open booking form:** Allow visitors to access the booking form.
- **US-08 — Enter guest name:** Collect the guest's name.
- **US-09 — Enter guest email/phone:** Collect contact information.
- **US-10 — Select room type:** Let visitors select their preferred room.
- **US-11 — Choose check-in date:** Collect the intended arrival date.
- **US-12 — Choose check-out date:** Collect the intended departure date.

### Proposed Sprint 2 Goal

Develop a usable booking form that allows visitors to enter their details, select a room type, choose check-in and check-out dates, and prepare a booking request for validation and processing.

### Dependencies and Considerations

- Confirm the booking form’s required fields.
- Validate that the check-out date is later than the check-in date.
- Coordinate the frontend with the backend and database team.
- Confirm how the frontend will submit booking data to the backend.
- Plan testing for invalid or missing information.
